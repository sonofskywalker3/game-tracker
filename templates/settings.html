Setup Instructions

  1. Go to dev.twitch.tv/console and log in (create account if needed)
  2. Click Register Your Application
  3. Fill in:
    • Name: Game Tracker
    • OAuth Redirect URLs: http://localhost
    • Category: Application Integration
  4. Click Create
  5. Click Manage on your new app
  6. Copy the Client ID
  7. Click New Secret to generate a Client Secret, then copy it
  8. Paste both values above and save
